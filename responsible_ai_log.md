## Why this matters

Junior engineers using an AI troubleshooting assistant could make incorrect
changes to live network devices if they blindly trust AI output. This log
demonstrates that:

1. A human reviewer evaluated every AI diagnosis before any fix was applied.
2. Cases where the AI was wrong, incomplete, or over-confident were caught
   and corrected — not silently accepted.
3. The team can point to concrete evidence of the human-in-the-loop safety
   rule being followed, not just claimed.
